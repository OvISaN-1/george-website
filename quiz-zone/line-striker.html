Line Striker 3D · TEST

← All games

Freeze. Draw. Score.

Draw the pass. Draw the shot.

Time stops while you plan. Draw a line with your finger and the ball follows it. You have three lives. Every goal makes the defenders quicker and the keeper smarter. Rebounds are live, so follow them up!

  • 1Time freezes. Look at where everyone is. Two fingers or the round buttons turn and zoom the camera.
  • 2Draw a line from George to a team-mate, or into the goal.
  • 3Curve it round the defenders. You can pass in any direction, even backwards. The line turns red when a defender is in the way.
  • 4Draw a long line for a lob or cross: a team-mate near goal can head it or even try a bicycle kick. Draw a long, hard line at the goal from the orange zone for a power shot. A longer line is a harder strike: harder to save or block, and harder for a team-mate to control. Hold still at the end of a pass to push it further. Aim for the corners. Hit the glowing top corner for bonus points. Wrong line? Let go in the red strip to cancel.
