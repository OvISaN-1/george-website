Pass, pass, pass... shoot

Three minutes. Score as many as you can.

You only attack. Pass your way through the defence and shoot. If a defender wins the ball you lose a couple of seconds and start a fresh attack.

  1. 1Drag to pass. Drag the way you want the ball to go. A longer drag is a longer pass. Green line = safe, red = a defender is in the way.
  2. 2Three passes. ⚽ Ground, ⤴ Lob over the defenders, ⚡ Through-ball to a runner.
  3. 3Hold to shoot. Hold SHOOT to build power and let go in the green. Slide across the goal to aim.
  4. 4Super skill. Pass your way to a full bar. A super shot is deadly close in, but the keeper can get to it from far out.

    Drag to pass.

    Drag from anywhere to pass; 1, 2, 3 pick Ground, Lob or Through. Hold the goal (or Space or the SHOOT button) to charge a shot, slide to aim, let go in the green. E or Shift fires your super skill. Arrow keys aim a pass or a shot.

    Top scores

    Score = 100 for a goal, plus bonuses for long chains of passes and long shots, plus 10 for every pass. Harder teams multiply your points. The whole game engine is written in Rust and runs in your browser as WebAssembly. Top scores are shared online with everyone who plays.
