Run. Jump. Collect.

The pitch never ends. Can George keep up?

George runs and runs, and he gets faster and faster. Grab every coin, dodge the referee's cards and pick up football gear for super powers. Four lives, then it's over.

  • 👈👉Swipe left or right to change lane. (Arrow keys or A and D on a keyboard.)
  • 👆Swipe up to jump over barriers. (Up arrow, W or Space.)
  • 👇Swipe down to slide under bars. (Down arrow or S.)
  • 🔟Your shirt number is your coin value. Every coin is worth your number, so pick up number boosts. A card knocks it down by 5.
⚽ Power-ups and cards
  • 🟨 Yellow cards: jump over them. 🟥 Red cards: go round them.
  • ⚽ Football: kicked ahead, it picks up every coin in front of you.
  • 👟 Golden boots: a sprint with double distance points.
  • 📣 Referee whistle: play slows down.
  • 🧤 Keeper gloves: a shield against one card.
  • 🏆 Golden trophy: coins fly to you.
  • 🚀 Rocket boots: fly over everything.
  • 🧣 Fan scarf: an extra life.
  • #️⃣ Number badges: +5, +10 or double your shirt number.
  • 🧑‍⚖️ Referee: jogs across two lanes. Use the third one.
  • 🦵 Defender: the red ! means he is about to slide in. Jump over him.
  • 🚜 Groundsman's mower: drives towards you. Go round it.
  • 💪 Keep playing: lost your last life? Once per run, spend 50 shop coins to carry on.
