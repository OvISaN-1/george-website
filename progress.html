💾 Save my progress

Your games save on this device only. Make a login and your progress follows you to any phone, tablet or computer. It covers Matchday (career, season, unlocks, what the game has learned), Free Kick Masters, badges and settings.

Your login

Pick a username and a password. Once you are logged in, your progress saves by itself while you play, and your scores go on the top-scores tables under your username, so you never have to type your name again. Log in on another device to get it all back.

Username: 3 to 18 letters, numbers, - or _. Password: at least 4 characters. Write your password down. There is no email, so Dad has to reset it if you forget. Please don't use your real name or a password you use anywhere else.
